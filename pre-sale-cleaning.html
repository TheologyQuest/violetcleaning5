BEFORE AND AFTER THE SALE

Different stages. Different cleaning needs.

Preparing a home for sale and cleaning it before moving in have different priorities. We’ll discuss what you need and tailor the clean to your property.

Pre-sale — preparing your home for buyers

Cleaning to help your home look its best for photography, inspections and open homes. We can focus on kitchens, bathrooms, floors, glass and other areas that need attention before the property goes on the market.

Post-sale — a thorough clean before moving in

A thorough clean of your newly purchased home, with attention to the areas you want cleaned and sanitised before moving in. Tell us your priorities, and we’ll agree on the rooms, surfaces and tasks to include.

The exact scope will depend on the property, its condition and what is agreed when quoting.
