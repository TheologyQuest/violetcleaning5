THE HANDOVER CLEAN

What can a bond clean cover?

Our bond cleaning checklist covers the areas most commonly included in a detailed handover clean. Open each area to see the typical tasks, then we can confirm the exact scope for your property when quoting.

The final scope depends on the property, its condition and what is agreed when quoting.

Before we arrive

  • Remove personal belongings from the rooms being cleaned.
  • Make sure power and running water are available.
  • Tell us about stains, damage or specialist requests when quoting.

What is not covered unless agreed?

Some tasks require specialist equipment, extra time or separate services. These may include stained or damaged blinds, sticker or hook removal, moving furniture, ceiling cleaning, specialist tile and grout cleaning, and other work outside the agreed scope. Full-wall scrubbing or washing is an additional service and must be agreed separately. Cleaning furniture, furnishings, appliances, kitchenware, linen and other household contents in furnished properties is not included unless agreed in advance. This may include couches, chairs, tables, bedside tables, entertainment units, lamps, outdoor furniture, bed frames and mattresses; refrigerators, microwaves, washing machines, dryers, toasters, kettles, coffee makers and other small kitchen appliances; and crockery, cutlery, glassware and utensils. Personal belongings should be removed before cleaning.
