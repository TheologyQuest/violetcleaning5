This is the typical scope for a detailed bond and end-of-lease clean. It is provided as a convenient reference for customers who would prefer to view or print the full scope in one place. The final scope depends on the property, its condition and what is agreed when quoting.

Kitchen

  • Stovetop, range hood and oven
  • Benchtops, splashbacks and sinks
  • Cupboards and drawers inside and out
  • Appliance exteriors
  • Windows, tracks and sills
  • Tiles, fittings, doors and skirting boards
  • Light switches, power points and accessible fittings
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped; cobwebs removed

Bedrooms

  • Mirrors, windows, tracks and sills
  • Doors, frames and tracks
  • Cupboards and blinds
  • Walls spot cleaned and skirting boards
  • Light switches, power points and accessible fittings
  • Carpets or hard floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Cobwebs removed

Bathrooms

  • Baths, showers, fittings and glass
  • Basins, vanities and toilets
  • Mirrors, windows and tracks
  • Accessible mould and exhaust fans
  • Tiles, cupboards, walls and skirting boards
  • Light switches and accessible fittings
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped; cobwebs removed

Laundry

  • Benchtops, sinks and fittings
  • Windows, tracks and sills
  • Cupboards, doors and skirting boards
  • Blinds, cobwebs and accessible fittings
  • Light switches and accessible fittings
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped

Stairs and common areas

  • Stairs, hallways and common areas
  • Windows, tracks and sills
  • Doors, frames and tracks
  • Skirting boards and walls spot cleaned
  • Cupboards and blinds where applicable
  • Light switches, power points and accessible fittings
  • Floors, fingerprints and cobwebs

Fans and air conditioners

  • Accessible covers wiped over
  • Filters cleaned where accessible and practical
  • Ceiling fans cleaned where accessible and practical
  • Cobwebs removed where accessible

Balconies and patios

  • General sweep and mop where suitable
  • Light fittings and fans where accessible
  • Railings wiped where accessible
  • Cobwebs removed where accessible

Garage area

  • General sweep and mop where suitable
  • Walls spot cleaned where applicable
  • Skirting boards and accessible fittings
  • Cupboards, windows and tracks where applicable
  • Cobwebs removed where accessible

Optional extras

  • Carpet steam cleaning
  • Upholstery or rug cleaning
  • External pressure cleaning
  • Full-wall scrubbing or washing
  • Rubbish removal
  • Handyman, lawn mowing or garden tidy
Before we arriveRemove personal belongings, arrange access, and make sure power and running water are available.
Not included unless agreedFurniture, household contents, stained or damaged blinds, sticker or hook removal, moving furniture, and specialist work require prior agreement.
