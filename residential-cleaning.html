HOW WE CAN HELP

What would you like cleaned?

Choose the type of clean that best describes what you need, or contact us to discuss a specific job.

Spring cleaning

A thorough refresh for your home, focusing on areas that need more attention than the usual household clean. Tell us what you’d like tackled, and we’ll plan the clean around your priorities.

Pre-inspection cleaning

Cleaning to help you prepare for a routine rental inspection by your real estate agent or property manager. Let us know the inspection date and the rooms or areas that need attention so we can check availability.

One-off deep cleaning

A more detailed clean based on your home’s condition and the areas you want addressed. We’ll discuss the tasks and level of cleaning required before providing a quote.

Individual rooms and tasks

Need an oven cleaned, a couple of bathrooms done or help with selected areas? We can discuss individual cleaning jobs without you needing to book a whole-house clean.

The exact scope and price will depend on the property, its condition and the tasks agreed when quoting.
